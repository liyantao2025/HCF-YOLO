Figure 5. Qualitative comparison of YOLO11n and HCF-YOLO11n in dense cotton seedling scenes. (a,c) Full-scene ground-truth annotations, predictions, and detection-derived response maps for a representative dense scene and a challenging scene, respectively. Dashed white rectangles indicate the regions enlarged in (b,d). (b,d) Matched local enlargements using identical crop coordinates across all columns. Ground-truth boxes are cyan, YOLO11n predictions are orange, and HCF-YOLO11n predictions are green. Numbered circles identify success- and failure-oriented examples: 1, a seedling missed by both models; 2, a case with a stronger HCF-YOLO11n response; 3-4, seedlings detected by both models; and 5-6, YOLO11n misses recovered by HCF-YOLO11n. Response maps were generated from confidence-weighted Gaussian kernels centered on predicted boxes (radius = max(3, 0.5 min(w,h)); sigma = max(radius/2,1)); overlapping responses were combined by the pixelwise maximum. For each scene, the two response fields were jointly normalized by their shared maximum and rendered with the same color map and [0,1] display range. No internal network activation layer was used. These panels are qualitative examples and are interpreted together with the quantitative detection metrics.
